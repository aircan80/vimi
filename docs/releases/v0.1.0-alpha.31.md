# VibeMiner v0.1.0-alpha.31

This update lets World creatures show their glowing and translucent parts. In
build 1.60.1.70862, creatures that drew nothing because all their surfaces were
glows or translucent parts now draw them, and creatures that already drew can
now show glowing and translucent parts too, including glows and veils whose
texture weight fades, taken at the idle pose the World draws. In the display
census, 962 of 1,050 sampled creature models now have a surface the World can
draw, up from 887 in alpha.30. Library storage did not change: no schema version or table changed
since alpha.30, so an alpha.30 library opens as it is, without migration. World
creature surface bindings are rebuilt once (#623, #625, #628).

The creature changes were measured on real data with the measurement probe's
display census and covered by unit tests, without a GPU proof of their own. The
owner's on-screen checks on Windows with Direct3D 11 are still pending for this
release and for alpha.30 and alpha.29, and nothing new was run on Metal; see
Known limits at the end.

## Creatures: glows and translucent parts in the World

Creatures stay opt-in in the World. The previews are unchanged. Counts are from
the display census over 1,050 creature models with a static preview in
1.60.1.70862.

- **Glows.** A section that no opaque or alpha-keyed batch binds now draws its
  first additive batch in the World objects' additive pass. Glow-only
  creatures now appear, and 117 models that already drew gain glowing parts
  such as eyes, auras and crystals. In the census, drawn models go from 887 to
  929, and refusals from 161 to 119. Glows are drawn unlit (#623).
- **Translucent parts.** A section that no opaque, alpha-keyed or additive batch
  binds now draws its first alpha-blended batch in the World objects' blended
  pass, lit unless the material is unlit. In the census, drawn models go from
  929 to 942, and refusals from 119 to 106; about 70 models that draw now show
  blended parts (#625).
- **Fading glows and veils.** A glow or blended section whose texture weight is
  animated now takes its weight at the sample the World poses creatures at:
  time 0 of the display's Stand sequence. A weight of 0 there hides the
  section, a full weight draws it as before, and a partial weight draws it
  with the texture's alpha scaled by the weight. A glow that pulses during
  Stand is drawn at its time-0 value. Creatures shown in their rest pose
  still leave fading glows and veils out, and so do creatures whose Stand
  plays from an external skeleton. In the census, drawn models go from 942
  to 962, and refusals from 106 to 86; 117 drawn models now draw at least one
  fading section, 55 of them with a partial weight. Many of the new glows sit
  on camera-facing bones, which the Stand pose still leaves out (#618, #628).
- In both cases an opaque or alpha-keyed batch still claims its section first,
  so surfaces that drew before are unchanged. Humanoid NPC appearances still
  leave additive and blended surfaces out (#623, #625).
- On a library that has not stored the newly used textures, those creatures are
  refused as having an unavailable texture until the files are stored (#623,
  #625, #628).

## Storage

Library storage is unchanged: no schema, user version or table changed since
alpha.30. The rule revision of the World creature surface bindings changed with
the glow, blended and fading-weight rules, so World creature bindings are
rebuilt once (#623, #625, #628). A binding is not saved while its Stand or
texture weights could still change on a later read, such as when the model or
skeleton file is missing or incomplete (#628, #630). Saved model previews are
not rebuilt.

## Measurement and tests

- The display census gained a breakdown of refused models by their fewest
  blockers (#623) and a count of drawn models by their glow and blended
  surfaces (#625), and a count of models by how their fading sections were
  taken at the Stand sample, on a cold run only (#628).
- New tests cover the glow and blended rules, the order in which a section's
  batches claim it, and a World binding that is not saved when a texture weight
  cannot be read (#623, #625).
- New tests cover fading weights at the Stand sample: hidden, full and
  partial weights, the played slice of an aliased Stand, global sequences,
  linear keys whose first key is after time 0, creatures without a Stand pose,
  undecided tracks, skeleton Stands, and a binding that is not saved while its
  Stand cannot be read (#628, #630).
- A World audio test allows a wider hand-over gap on a busy test machine while
  still failing when the next file's open is on the critical path (#629).

## Build

- The Gradle wrapper waits up to 60 s instead of 10 s on the network when it
  downloads Gradle (#624). Nothing in the app changed.

## Known limits

- **Owner checks on Windows.** The glow, blended and fading-weight rules (#623,
  #625, #628) were measured on real data with the display census and covered
  by unit tests, without a GPU proof of their own. These on-screen checks on Windows with
  Direct3D 11 are still pending:
  - glow-only creatures appearing as soft glows that add light, with no dark
    boxes or fully opaque quads; glows on creatures that already drew, with
    nothing showing that the client hides; and unlit glows that look too
    bright at night (#623);
  - translucent creatures and parts with no dark halos, fully opaque quads or
    parts showing through solid geometry; lit blended parts by day and at
    night; and blended parts of one creature flickering or popping in front of
    each other as the camera turns (#625);
  - partially faded glows and veils looking as bright or as faint as in the
    client's idle, with no dark or black halos; creatures that were missing,
    such as spirits, wisps and elementals, now appearing; and nothing showing
    that the client hides in idle (#628).

  Carried over from alpha.30 and still pending:
  - creatures that used to stand frozen now idling, with nothing jumping,
    flipping, exploding or tilting, including alias Stands, bone-file rigs and
    skeleton rigs (face and hands) (#607, #609, #614, #615);
  - glow cards pulsing or drifting while staying camera-facing in the previews
    (#616);
  - World creatures with flames or glows standing with those parts left out
    (#618);
  - World creatures that were missing now drawn with their base texture, and
    how far the missing shine, tints and fades show (#620);
  - upright sprites in the preview from eye level and from above (#619);
  - switching between Near and Far on the Display page, the remembered choice,
    and the disabled choice with the launch flag (#611);
  - Far filling smoothly and clicking an outer-ring tile naming the zone
    (#608);
  - the zone text and its music right at spawn (#610).

  Carried over from alpha.29 and still pending:
  - the stretched non-square textures and the formerly untextured tiles (#576,
    #572);
  - water and lava: recorded ocean and river colours, coasts, and opaque lava,
    fel and tar (#586, #594);
  - the recorded light on terrain, objects and the horizon, by day and at night
    (#591, #592, #597);
  - glass and water panes in one object drawing in the right order (#574);
  - creatures' body parts under the new geoset default (#599);
  - the recorded sky in native window presentation (#567), whose proof has not
    been run on Windows yet;
  - splat blending with `-Dvimi.world.splat=true`: the fade band staying put
    while turning, no seam from a neighbouring chunk, and no tiles stuck
    untextured on the high tier at Near (#581, #583);
  - ambience starting without an extra pause when flying between zones (#578).

  The Metal shaders are built, and nothing new was run on macOS hardware.
- **Creatures still in the rest pose.** In the previews, billboard bones with
  child bones or rotation keys, members not rigidly bound, lock-X cards and a
  few parent-inheritance flags still refuse the Stand. In the World, 3 models
  with ignore-parent flags and the two models above the bone limit stay in the
  rest pose. Models whose Stand records no bone keys show their rest pose
  (#384).
- **Creatures not drawn in the World.** 86 of 1,050 census models still have no
  surface the World can draw. By their fewest blockers: 34 have glow or
  blended batches whose texture weight fades, 25 use character texture slots
  on displays without extended appearance, 11 have empty monster skin slots,
  6 use edge-fade or environment combiners, 9 have no batch blocker but lose
  their sections to geosets or hidden sections, and 1 has an unavailable
  texture. Of the 34 with fading weights, 14 play their Stand from an external
  skeleton, 1 has no Stand pose, 4 also have an edge-fade combiner or an empty
  monster skin slot, and 14 take a full or partial weight but still draw
  nothing visible, in most of those checked because the display's geosets
  hide the drawn sections (#361, #384, #628).
- **Glow and blended parts in the World.** Colour tracks are not applied as
  tints or alpha, so a blended part can draw more opaque than in the client.
  Glows are drawn unlit; about 10% of the sampled glow batches are not marked
  unlit, so those draw brighter at night than in the client. Blended parts sort
  per part within a creature, so overlapping parts can sort wrongly from some
  angles. A creature shown in its rest pose draws its camera-facing glow and
  blended quads fixed. Fading weights are taken at time 0 of Stand, so a glow
  that pulses or fades during Stand stays at that value, and a partial weight
  scales the alpha up to 8-bit rounding. Keyed weights on models whose Stand
  plays from an external skeleton are still left out, as are fading weights on
  creatures in the rest pose (#361, #623, #625, #628).
- **Bone files** are not applied for character displays, and `.anim` files and
  parent skeletons are not read (#384).
- **Sun direction.** The light's direction is still fixed. The light tables
  record colours, not a direction, and the client's sun path is not pinned yet
  (#177).
- **Water depth.** The shallow-to-deep mix reads the depth byte linearly; the
  client's exact curve and water textures are not used yet (#594).
- **View distance.** Near stays the default; whether Far becomes the default is
  not decided (#173).
- **Opt-in features.** These are Java system properties for testing, not
  settings in the app yet:
  - splat blending: `-Dvimi.world.splat=true`;
  - the recorded sky, fog, light and water light: `-Dvimi.world.sky=recorded`,
    with `-Dvimi.world.sky.time=HH:MM` to pin a time;
  - creatures: `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`;
  - WMO vertex colours: `-Dvimi.world.wmoVertexColours=true`;
  - high object texture detail: `-Dvimi.world.objectTextures=high`;
  - native presentation into a child window, on Windows only:
    `-Dvimi.world.nativePresent=true`.

  `-Dvimi.world.viewDistance=far` still forces the Far view distance. Terrain
  lighting is on and can be turned off with
  `-Dvimi.world.terrainLighting=false`.
- **Outland and Northrend** (maps 530 and 571) are not in build 1.60.1.70862,
  so the texture fixes could not be measured there.
- **NPCs.** On 1.60.1.70862, few spawned NPCs have a Creature record, so few
  reach a display, as in alpha.30.
- **macOS:** packages are unsigned and not notarized, and native presentation is
  not available. The signature check on `--image-only` builds was tested with
  `codesign` stubbed and has not been run on a Mac yet (#568).
- **Linux:** packages need the standard GTK and X11 desktop libraries, and native
  presentation is not available. The deb dependencies added in alpha.29 were
  not tested with a clean install.
