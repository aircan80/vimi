# VibeMiner v0.1.0-alpha.8

Races adds narrow Tauren male/female static appearance profiles alongside the
reviewed Human and Orc profiles. Body, hair and fur atlases use existing bounded
composition and qualified source textures; recorded starting outfits retain their
own geometry and material choices.

Admission is restricted to Tauren race 6, models 11/12, layouts 113/114 and matching
sexes. Two unverified compatibility slots are checked only as exact-layout literal
zeros within this profile. Their names remain outside the general allowlist;
nonzero or differently shaped data is unsupported, with no fallback traversal.

Four actual Forever 1.60.1.70707 samples covered both sexes with recorded Warrior
and Druid outfits. Every selected BLP decoded, and all four results restored from
pack storage after recreating the service. These are lowest recorded eligible
samples, not client-default customization or account-unlock claims.

Previews remain static and partial. Unsupported eye/accessory bindings and
attachments retain neutral surfaces and explicit warnings, including cache hits.
No Human boot/hoof rule or sleeve geometry is invented for Tauren. Animation,
held equipment and other race/model/layout profiles remain future work.

The derived appearance policy changes once, so affected previews regenerate under
the existing build-owned storage. Original CDN objects are retained; no storage
migration or data reset is introduced. Independent architecture/domain review and
29 component tests passed. Native CI and packaged checks must pass before publishing.
#104 remains open for its remaining capabilities.
