# VibeMiner v0.1.0-alpha.33

This update draws caves and building interiors on open-world maps. Before, the
World skipped every WMO interior group outside dungeons and raids. It also
draws 15 more World creatures on Classic Era, build 1.60.1.70862, and the World
Explorer now starts with music and ambience on. The release also tidies a few
checks from alpha.32's Anniversary work, with no change in behaviour.
Library storage did not change: no schema version or table changed since
alpha.32, so an alpha.32 library opens as it is, without migration. Saved
creature previews and World creature bindings are rebuilt once, because their
rules changed (#636, #637, #638, #639, #640, #641).

The changes were measured on real data with the World measurement probe on
build 1.60.1.70862 and covered by unit tests. The World object GPU proofs,
including the portal-cull proof, pass on Vulkan on Linux. The owner's on-screen
checks on Windows with Direct3D 11 are still pending for this release and for
alpha.32, alpha.31, alpha.30 and alpha.29, and nothing new was run on Metal;
see Known limits at the end.

## Caves and building interiors

- **Interiors on open-world maps.** The World now draws WMO interior groups on
  every map. Dungeons and raids keep their existing instance path, which reads
  interiors inline with the exteriors, unchanged. Every other map, including
  continents, battlegrounds and arenas, uses the new open-world path, so caves
  and building interiors show. Portal sets are carried for these buildings, so
  from outside, the renderer's portal culling hides an interior unless it is
  seen through a portal (#641).
- **Exteriors first.** On open-world maps a building's interior groups are no
  longer read with its exterior groups. They are read in a second pass, after
  every building's exterior has been read and admitted, from what is left of
  the tile's 256 group reads. Each building's interiors are read whole or not
  at all. When they do not fit, they are counted as `GROUP_INTERIOR_CAPPED`
  and that building draws its exterior without a portal set, as before. Draw,
  mesh, geometry and slot budgets were already given to exteriors first. No
  exterior root is newly capped by interiors in the measured areas (#641).
- **Image levels.** Exterior images are given their levels first, from the
  pool minus a small reserve for the smallest level of every image that only
  interiors draw: about 1/30 of a 512 BC1 chain per interior image. Interior
  images then take what is left, and every interior image keeps at least its
  smallest level. This also applies on dungeon and raid maps (#641).
- **Unreadable interiors.** An interior group or image that cannot be read
  right now leaves out only that group or part and never fails the tile's
  buildings. The same failure on an exterior image still fails them, as
  before (#641).
- **Spirit Rise.** In the Spirit Rise cave in Thunder Bluff (root 113816, on
  tiles 1:33,31 and 1:34,31), only group 0 was drawn, with no portal set.
  Groups 0, 1, 2 and 3 are now drawn, with a portal set of 4 groups and 3
  portals (#641).
- **Per-tile results.** Measured in the probe's prep mode on one tile, repeat
  pass, with interiors off and on:

  | Tile | Groups drawn | Interior groups capped | Group reads | Tile prep time |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Thunder Bluff 1:34,31 | 61 → 75 | 0 → 1 | 61 → 76 | 1248 → 1386 ms (+11%) |
  | Orgrimmar 1:29,40 | 5 → 48 | 0 → 107 | 5 → 155 | 1111 → 1310 ms (+18%) |
  | Undercity 0:29,31 | 19 → 22 | 0 → 193 | 19 → 216 | 1110 → 1358 ms (+22%) |
  | Ironforge 0:41,33 | 2 → 21 | 0 → 85 | 2 → 106 | 1067 → 1467 ms (+37%) |
  | Goldshire 0:49,31 | 5 → 16 | 0 → 0 | 11 → 22 | 1220 → 1340 ms (+10%) |

  Most of the added time is spent reading interior groups that are then capped
  by the building geometry budget the exteriors leave (#641).
- **Streaming.** In stream mode on the GPU, 3x3 tiles at Near around the city
  with the camera outside, Orgrimmar went from 26 to 303 groups drawn, with a
  mean of 188 portal meshes culled per frame, and its buildings settled in
  15.8 s instead of 13.8 s. Undercity went from 109 to 193 groups drawn and
  settled in 16.5 s instead of 15.3 s. Mean draws per frame rose from 466 to
  512 in Orgrimmar and from 879 to 903 in Undercity (#641).

## Fewer wasted reads in cities

- **Interior groups skipped before reading.** The root file of a WMO lists
  each group's flags (MOGI). On open-world maps the World now skips groups
  that are marked interior there before reading them, so they no longer use up
  the tile's group reads. A group that is read still follows its own flags, as
  before. Around Orgrimmar, 9 tiles read 26 group files instead of 889, and
  around Stormwind 85 instead of 144. The buildings drawn are the same: every
  other count in the probe was identical before and after (#640).
- **Roots with up to 512 groups.** The root limit went from 256 to 512 groups.
  Stormwind is one building with 289 groups, 280 of them interior, and its
  root failed to parse on every tile that places it. It now parses, but it is
  still not drawn; see Known limits (#640).

## World creatures

- **External-skeleton fades.** A model whose Stand plays from an external
  skeleton (`.skel`) had every keyed texture weight refused. Such a weight is
  now decided as full when the model has no sequences or global sequences of
  its own, the track runs on sequence time with step or linear keys, and every
  keyed slice starts at full weight. Every other weight track of such a model
  is still refused, with a more specific reason when a slice does not start
  full. All 21 external-skeleton creature models that reach this step on build
  1.60.1.70862 have this shape (#636).
- **Object skin slots.** A model's object skin texture slot now takes the
  display's first texture variation when every other texture on the model is
  a file. The World and the previews use the same rule. It is inferred from the
  tables, not from client code, and limited to the shape seen in this build. The
  slot stays unbound on models with a monster skin slot or any character slot,
  and when the first variation is empty (#639).
- **Census.** In the probe's display census of 1,051 sampled creature models
  on build 1.60.1.70862, 963 were drawn before these changes, 978 after the
  external-skeleton fix and 979 after the object skin rule. Models with no
  surface the World can draw went from 86 to 70. Of the 3 displays the object
  skin rule now resolves, two name textures that were not stored in the
  measured local copy; they should draw wherever those files are stored
  (#636, #639).

## Music and ambience

The World Explorer's Music and Ambience toggles now start on instead of off.
Nothing plays until the current terrain area is known, so a world never starts
playing early. Either can still be turned off from the HUD icons or the game
menu (#638).

## Smaller fixes

- **WDT directory check.** The map catalog and the map binding now share one
  check before building a WDT path from a map's directory: 1 to 64 ASCII
  letters, digits or underscores. The catalog used to accept a directory
  longer than 64 characters that the binding would then refuse, so the two can
  no longer disagree. The saved map binding stamp is unchanged (#637).
- **Paletted handmade mips.** The terrain stream's guard for handmade mip
  chains now uses one shared check, and a test keeps it equal to the texture
  decoder's rule for every combination (#637).

## Storage

Library storage is unchanged: no schema, user version or table changed since
alpha.32. Two binding stamps changed, so saved bindings are rebuilt once on
first use:

- the Stand weight revision went to `m2-stand-texture-weights-v2`, which is
  part of every saved World creature binding (#636);
- the creature preview policy went to v6 and the World creature policy to v5,
  so saved creature previews and World creature bindings are rebuilt (#639).

The WMO root facts revision went to `wmo17-facts-v2` because root facts now
include the group flags. The tile objects file layout is unchanged, and those
files are written per session, so no saved objects tile outlives the change
(#640). The map binding stamp did not change (#637).

## Measurement and tests

- The World measurement probe draws interiors as the World view does.
  `-Pprobe.interiors=false` measures exteriors only, for a before and after on
  the same build, and in prep mode `-Pprobe.root=<FileDataID>` lists one
  root's drawn groups and portal set on each tile (#641).
- New probe series: `objects.groups.readPerTile`, the group files read per
  tile (#640), and `objects.groups.interiorReadPerTile`, the interior pass's
  share of it (#641).
- The probe's display census applies the object skin rule and reports
  `empty object skin slot` as its own refusal (#639).
- New tests cover open-world interiors: interiors opened only after every
  exterior while dungeon interiors are read inline, interior reads never
  capping a later exterior root, an interior-only root that does not fit being
  capped, portal sets carried for deferred interiors and dropped when their
  reads are capped, exterior image levels identical with and without interiors
  when the reserve does not bind, and an unreadable interior group or image
  never costing the exterior (#641).
- New tests cover the root group flags: a 512-group root parses and 513 is
  refused, the flags kept in group order, flags dropped without refusing the
  root when they are absent, mismatched, partial, duplicated or over budget,
  and skipped interior groups still counted and leaving room for later roots
  (#640).
- New tests cover external-skeleton weights, decided for full single-slice and
  full-first-key tracks and refused otherwise (#636), and the object skin
  rule, in the previews and end to end in a World binding (#639).
- New tests pin the WDT directory check in the binding and the catalog, the
  handmade mip guard against the decoder, a table whose inline ID is stored
  after array fields, and Music and Ambience starting on (#637, #638).

## Known limits

- **Interiors in cities are partial.** In the big cities most interior groups
  are read and then capped by the building geometry budget the exteriors
  leave, and counted as `GROUP_INTERIOR_CAPPED`. Undercity's exteriors already
  use 273,669 of its 294,912 building indices. Raising that budget is a
  separate decision. On one Undercity tile, roots whose portal set was dropped
  draw unculled, with only the few interior groups that fit (#641).
- **Interior lighting.** Interiors are not lit with interior lighting yet.
- **Stormwind is still not drawn.** Its root parses since this release and
  only its exterior groups are read, but those exceed the per-tile building
  geometry budget, so the root is capped on every tile (#640).
- **Exterior image levels.** The reserve for interior images can lower an
  exterior image's level slightly, by at most the smallest level of each
  interior-only image (#641).
- **No switch for interiors.** Interiors are always drawn; the app has no
  setting to turn them off. Only the probe can measure without them (#641).
- **No World creatures on Anniversary.** There is no reviewed source of
  creature spawns for build 2.5.6.70577, so `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`
  shows no creatures there.
- **Humanoid NPC appearances on Anniversary.** The tables that describe
  humanoid NPC appearances have not been reviewed for that build.
- **Sky rows skipped on Anniversary.** 12 LightData rows are skipped because
  their colour values have a top byte that is set, such as `0xFF242141`, and
  what that byte means on this build is not established. In 10 rows, from two
  parameter sets, the sky colours are unreadable and the whole row is skipped.
  In 2 rows, from two other parameter sets, only the direct and ambient light
  are dropped and the sky and fog are kept (#634).
- **Other layouts.** Only the Anniversary Map, LightParams and LightData
  layouts added in alpha.32 are reviewed. Other classic layouts are refused
  until they are seen and reviewed (#632, #634).
- **Owner checks on Windows.** The changes were measured with the probe and
  covered by unit tests. These on-screen checks on Windows with Direct3D 11

  The Metal shaders are built, and nothing new was run on macOS hardware.
- **Creatures still in the rest pose.** In the previews, billboard bones with
  child bones or rotation keys, members not rigidly bound, lock-X cards and a
  few parent-inheritance flags still refuse the Stand. In the World, 3 models
  with ignore-parent flags and the two models above the bone limit stay in the
  rest pose. Models whose Stand records no bone keys show their rest pose
  (#384).
- **Creatures not drawn in the World.** On Classic Era, 70 of the 1,051 sampled
  census models still have no surface the World can draw, down from 86, and 2
  have visibility unresolved. The remaining refusals include 19 character race
  displays with no table naming their skin, which need character composition
  (#385); 3 displays with an empty object skin slot; and 3 whose texture is not
  stored in the measured local copy. Two external-skeleton models are still
  not drawn because of other blockers (#636, #639).
- **Glow and blended parts in the World.** Colour tracks are not applied as
  tints or alpha, so a blended part can draw more opaque than in the client.
  Glows are drawn unlit, so glow batches not marked unlit draw brighter at
  night than in the client. Blended parts sort per part within a creature, so
  overlapping parts can sort wrongly from some angles. A creature shown in its
  rest pose draws its camera-facing glow and blended quads fixed. Fading
  weights are taken at time 0 of Stand, so a glow that pulses or fades during
  Stand stays at that value. On models whose Stand plays from an external
  skeleton, keyed weights are decided only when every slice starts full and
  the model has no sequences of its own; the others are still left out, as are
  fading weights on creatures in the rest pose. Weight tracks are not read from
  `.anim` files or a parent skeleton (#361, #623, #625, #628, #636).
- **Bone files** are not applied for character displays, and `.anim` files and
  parent skeletons are not read (#384).
- **Sun direction.** The light's direction is still fixed. The light tables
  record colours, not a direction, and the client's sun path is not pinned yet
  (#177).
- **Water depth.** The shallow-to-deep mix reads the depth byte linearly; the
  client's exact curve and water textures are not used yet (#594).
- **View distance.** Near stays the default; whether Far becomes the default is
  not decided (#173).
- **Opt-in features.** These are Java system properties for testing, not
  settings in the app yet:
  - splat blending: `-Dvimi.world.splat=true`;
  - the recorded sky, fog, light and water light: `-Dvimi.world.sky=recorded`,
    with `-Dvimi.world.sky.time=HH:MM` to pin a time;
  - creatures: `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`;
  - WMO vertex colours: `-Dvimi.world.wmoVertexColours=true`;
  - high object texture detail: `-Dvimi.world.objectTextures=high`;
  - native presentation into a child window, on Windows only:
    `-Dvimi.world.nativePresent=true`.

  `-Dvimi.world.viewDistance=far` still forces the Far view distance. Terrain
  lighting is on and can be turned off with
  `-Dvimi.world.terrainLighting=false`.
- **Outland and Northrend on Classic Era.** Maps 530 and 571 are not in build
  1.60.1.70862. Outland is measured on Anniversary only, and Northrend was
  not measured. The interior changes were measured on Classic Era only.
- **NPCs.** On 1.60.1.70862, few spawned NPCs have a Creature record, so few
  reach a display, as in alpha.32.
- **macOS:** packages are unsigned and not notarized, and native presentation is
  not available. The signature check on `--image-only` builds was tested with
  `codesign` stubbed and has not been run on a Mac yet (#568).
- **Linux:** packages need the standard GTK and X11 desktop libraries, and native
  presentation is not available. The deb dependencies added in alpha.29 were
  not tested with a clean install.
