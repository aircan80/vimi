# VibeMiner v0.1.0-alpha.44

This update brings the World Explorer's NPCs to life and lets you swim and
fly. It replaces alpha.43, which was tagged but never published: its Linux
packages failed on a timing-dependent test, fixed here (#750). With `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`, NPCs near the camera now play their
idle animation, and each tile's buildings appear first while its NPCs follow,
so Stormwind's buildings are ready in about 11 s instead of about 2 minutes,
and on a repeat visit its NPCs finish arriving in about 30 s.
Walk mode can swim, in lakes and rivers and in building water such as the
Stormwind canals, which are now drawn too, and fly mode draws a gryphon. On
the Races page, 101 of the 116 race variants are now textured where their
texture files are stored, up from 89.
Library storage did not change: no schema version or table changed since
alpha.42, so an alpha.42 library opens as it is, without migration. This
release covers #718 to #755.

The changes were measured on real data on Classic Era, build 1.60.1.70862,
and covered by unit tests. The GPU proofs pass on Vulkan on Linux, and the
World appearance proof runs on Linux again. The owner's on-screen checks on
Windows with Direct3D 11 are still pending for this release and for alpha.42
back to alpha.29, and nothing new was run on Metal; see Known limits at the
end.

## World NPCs

These changes apply with `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`.

- **Idle animations.** Creatures and humanoid NPCs within 100 yd of the camera
  play their idle. Each model's pose is staggered, so a crowd doesn't move in
  step, and animation is capped at 384 placements and 100,000 posed vertices
  a frame, with its memory counted in the objects budget. Models with blended
  or glowing surfaces stay still. `-Dvimi.world.creatureAnimation=false`
  turns it off (#726, #741).
- **No stutter while turning.** All poses used to step on the same 40 ms
  tick, so some frames re-posed every NPC at once. Steps are now spread
  across frames: Stormwind at FAR went from 28.8 ms to 18.3 ms at the 95th
  percentile, against 18.1 ms with NPCs off (#738).
- **Buildings first.** Each tile's buildings are delivered first and its NPCs
  arrive in a later pass that replaces the drawn set. New buildings coming
  into view jump ahead of an NPC pass that is still preparing. Around
  Stormwind, buildings are ready in about 11 s instead of 123 s (#734).
- **Faster repeat visits.** Around Stormwind, NPCs finish arriving in about
  30 s on a repeat visit, down from about 114 s:
  - each humanoid NPC's idle is kept in the generated store between sessions,
    and animation sizes are carried rather than recomputed (#735, #742, #740);
  - NPCs with a helmet or shoulders whose saved appearance predates the
    appearance index are composed again once, so they are indexed from then
    on (#744, #752);
  - display choices keep each display's table chain for the session and read
    a tile's client records together (#748);
  - a tile's NPC pass reuses the buildings its buildings pass prepared, within
    64 MiB (#753, #754);
  - while one display is converted, the next two displays' remote parts are
    asked for ahead, with the same NPCs drawn as asking in turn (#755);
  - a carried idle's serialized form is checked once per animation (#749).

  The first session after updating takes longer, about 100 to 130 s around
  Stormwind, while saved NPC data is rebuilt.
- **Smaller fixes.** No idle is prepared before the camera is placed (#736).
  A busy NPC preview is asked again a few times before its tile's objects
  are delivered (#722), and a cancelled tile stops waiting for an NPC
  appearance (#718).

## Walk and fly

- **Swimming.** A walking body starts swimming once it is 0.6 of its height
  below the water surface. It swims at 4.722 yd/s forward and 2.5 yd/s
  backwards, floats at the surface, dives where you look, and rises with
  `Space`. It walks out only where the shore is walkable, so a steep bank
  works like a wall. The murloc plays its swim animations (#732).
- **Building water.** Water stored in buildings, such as the Stormwind
  canals, is read and placed with the building, so you can swim in it (#737),
  and it is drawn in the objects pass with the same water look as lakes and
  rivers. Interior water follows the rooms you can see (#739). Swimming takes
  the water's kind from the same tables as drawing, so the canals count as
  water (#746).
- **The gryphon.** Fly mode draws the gryphon, flapping as it flies, and walk
  mode keeps the murloc. Flying down onto walkable ground lands and stands;
  the walk/fly key switches back to walking. A body that fails to load is
  tried again after a short wait (#733).

## Races page

- 101 of the 116 race variants are textured where their texture files are
  stored, up from 89:
  - NPC races can use their NPC-only skins (#725);
  - vehicles whose layout declares texture canvases they never draw on are
    no longer refused (Delver's Dirigible, Delver's Gob-Trotter, Ethereal
    Airship) (#727);
  - the Cliffside Wylderdrake is textured (#728);
  - the Skeleton male draws the texture its model names directly (#730).
- A preview that could not be read in full says "Preview incomplete" and why,
  and is not saved (#724).
- A race whose base skin texture can't be read is refused with that reason
  instead of drawing a black body (#725).
- A batch whose texture-transform entry names no declared track is left
  undrawn everywhere rather than drawn with a guessed transform (#728).

## Reliability

- **A hung World renderer is caught.** The renderer process sends a
  heartbeat; if a command waits 30 s with no sign of progress, the session
  fails and offers Retry, naming what it was waiting for. A frame being
  presented to a native window gets up to 5 minutes, so minimising the
  window does not trip it (#723).
- **Startup on a busy machine.** World startup now fails only after 30 s
  with no sign of progress, at most 2 minutes in all, instead of a fixed
  10 s (#720).
- **Ambience fades.** Changing ambience exactly as its sound file looped cut
  the old track instead of fading it out (#743).

## Storage

Library storage is unchanged: no schema, user version or table changed since
alpha.42. The generated store gains entries for NPC idles; player race
previews, stored idles and the NPC appearance index are rebuilt once after
updating, because their identities changed (#730, #735, #742, #744).

## Build and tests

- The Java/C# interop tooling is removed, and the storage crash tests run in
  their own workflow (#721).
- The World appearance proof runs on Linux again, with new Vulkan
  references and a comparison that allows edges to fall a pixel apart but
  still catches a thin line appearing or disappearing (#731).
- Timing-dependent tests are now deterministic, including the World
  streaming tests that stopped alpha.43's Linux packages and an allocation
  check on macOS (#743, #750, #751), and the races census probe gains model
  dumps and render framing (#729). A test-only launch overload is renamed
  (#745).

## Known limits

- **A split building follows its tiles.** Each group of a building drawn by
  group is drawn only while the tile that owns it is ready. If that tile fails
  to prepare, its groups are missing while the rest of the building draws.
  More buildings are drawn this way, including Orgrimmar, Undercity and
  Ironforge (#652), and the largest buildings of Naxxramas and the Temple of
  Ahn'Qiraj (#674).
- **Extra reads for sizing.** A border-crossing building with interiors on an
  open-world map reads its interior groups once more to size them; a building
  that still fits reads them again in the interior pass. Around Orgrimmar
  this was a mean of about 21 reads per tile (at most 53), and about 6 to 8
  around Undercity and Ironforge; preparation still got faster (#652). When
  a building moves to the shared allowance, up to 8 of its groups already
  being read ahead are read again (#659). On instance maps, an owned interior
  whose held copy was dropped is read a second time when it is drawn (#674,
  #675).
- **Building and doodad sharpness.** The pixel budgets did not grow with the
  image caps, so on busy tiles more images share the same pixels and may get
  lower levels. Doodad images may drop to 16 pixels when the budget is tight
  (#656, #662, #664).
- **Interiors on one copy only.** When a building is drawn whole by two
  tiles, the tile drawing it from its own pool can add interior groups, from
  its own pool and, since #666, its interior allowance, that the other tile's
  copy leaves out. Those groups show only while that tile is ready.
- **Unculled roots.** In alpha.35, on one Undercity tile, roots whose portal
  set was dropped drew unculled, with only the few interior groups that fit.
  Whether this still happens now that Undercity is drawn by group was not
  re-measured (#641, #652). Buildings drawn whole from the shared allowance
  draw no interiors without their portal set (#659).
- **Tied set doodads.** Culling trusts each group's MODR. A doodad that
  sticks out through a doorway can be hidden while its outside part would be
  in view. A doodad listed by several groups stays untied and draws whenever
  its placement is in view. For a building drawn by group, another tile's
  group could also list a doodad tied here (#649).
- **Draws on the densest tile.** A tile draws at most 8,192 times. On Gilneas
  34,29 doodads take 7,947 of them (#662).
- **Read-ahead may read what a tile then skips.** Images are read ahead
  within the tile's image caps and read limits, but an image of a model the
  tile later caps, or of a surface it later drops, may still be read and then
  discarded: at most 2 extra reads per run measured (#671, #672).
- **A share needs room.** A tile draws its own share of a placement a
  neighbour splits only if it has a free building model, a placement slot,
  draws and room in the large allowance. Without them, that share is capped
  and the placement's groups on this tile are missing; its set doodads still
  draw (#661). A share with room only for its exteriors leaves its interior
  groups on this tile undrawn (#667).
- **Textures shared only when identical.** Two tiles share a texture only when
  they upload the same level in the same format; a tile that picked a smaller
  level uploads its own copy. When two sets upload the same texture at once,
  the second keeps its own copy, which is shared once the first is released
  (#660, #669).
- **Texture limit.** At Far, a set that needs more textures than are left
  waits until another set is released, so its tile's objects appear later.
  No set waited in the windows measured since sharing. Objects delivered with
  a tile's terrain that miss the limit are dropped for that tile, which keeps
  its terrain, and are not offered again; the app itself always delivers
  objects separately (#658, #660, #669).
- **Images still read per tile.** Sharing is on the GPU only: every tile's
  images are still written, read and hashed (#660).
- **Allowance models.** An untied doodad can reuse a model read through the
  interior allowance, and that model's images are then given levels with the
  other doodad images. On a tile already at its doodad pixel budget, the
  allowance models' images get a low resolution (#649).
- **Far objects budget.** The densest Far window measured, around Gilneas,
  reserves at most 161 MiB of the 212 MiB Standard budget (#666).
- **Each owning tile walks the whole set.** Each tile that owns part of a
  building drawn by group walks its whole portal set every frame. Measured at
  Far, measured on this build, this costs at most 0.07 ms of CPU per frame on
  average (Stormwind: 28 walks over about 820 groups; 0.18 ms at the 95th
  percentile), under 1% of the frame (#644).
- **A second placement of a split building.** A building is decided once per
  tile by its largest placement. A second placement of a building that this
  tile itself draws by group is capped (#643). Further placements of a
  building drawn whole draw the same model (#659), and a placement that
  crosses a tile border draws its own share, or draws whole if the building
  fits an empty tile (#661).
- **Instance maps.** On instance maps, a building over the tile's remaining
  group reads is still capped before it is sized, so it is not drawn (#643).
  No measured instance map hit this: the largest building has 92 groups,
  against 256 group reads per tile (#674).
- **Instance interiors flagged only by their group.** A group that its own
  flags call interior but the building's group list does not is drawn only
  when the building is drawn whole, never when it is drawn by group, and such
  groups are left out of the sizing on purpose (#674, #675).
- **Identical placement records.** The owner rule assumes every tile that
  lists a placement holds an identical MODF record for it; tiles whose records
  differed would pick different owners (#643).
- **Interior lighting.** Interiors are lit from their raw vertex colours, and
  the client's exact formula is not pinned (#474): the alpha term of the
  client's colour adjustment is not applied, and MOHD flags 0x2 and 0x8 are not
  honoured. If the client does add some ambient, interiors can look darker than
  in the client; the two darkest seen in Thunder Bluff are root 113750 group 1
  and root 113146 group 2. Doorway (trans) batches get full interior light,
  with no blend to the sun. A group flagged both exterior and interior is lit as
  interior, where the client may light it as outdoor. There is no sky and ground
  split of the ambient, and the building's own lights are not read. Set doodads
  marked to use interior light (MODD 0x2) are still lit by the sun (#383, #293,
  #646).
- **Exterior image levels.** The reserve for interior images can lower an
  exterior image's level slightly, by at most the smallest level of each
  interior-only image (#641).
- **No switch for interiors.** Interiors are always drawn; no setting turns
  off only interiors. `-Dvimi.world.exteriors=false` still turns off all
  buildings. Only the probe can measure exteriors without interiors (#641).
- **No World creatures on Anniversary.** There is no reviewed source of
  creature spawns for build 2.5.6.70577, so `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`
  shows no creatures there.
- **Humanoid NPC appearances on Anniversary.** The tables that describe
  humanoid NPC appearances have not been reviewed for that build.
- **Sky rows skipped on Anniversary.** 12 LightData rows are skipped because
  their colour values have a top byte that is set, such as `0xFF242141`, and
  what that byte means on this build is not established. In 10 rows, from two
  parameter sets, the sky colours are unreadable and the whole row is skipped.
  In 2 rows, from two other parameter sets, only the direct and ambient light
  are dropped and the sky and fog are kept (#634).
- **Other layouts.** Only the Anniversary Map, LightParams and LightData
  layouts added in alpha.32 are reviewed. Other classic layouts are refused
  until they are seen and reviewed (#632, #634).

- **Creatures still in the rest pose.** In the previews, billboard bones with
  rotation keys, lock-X cards and a few parent-inheritance flags still refuse
  the Stand. Camera-facing billboard bones with children or partly weighted
  members are posed for the camera in the previews only; the World keeps its
  current rule. In the World, 3 models with ignore-parent flags stay in the
  rest pose. Models whose Stand records no bone keys, such as the Skeleton
  female race model, show their rest pose (#384, #687).
- **Creatures not drawn in the World.** On Classic Era, 70 of the 1,051 sampled
  census models still have no surface the World can draw, and 2 have
  visibility unresolved. The remaining refusals include 19 character race
  displays with no table naming their skin, which need character composition
  (#385); 3 displays with an empty object skin slot; and 3 whose texture is not
  stored in the measured local copy. Two external-skeleton models are still
  not drawn because of other blockers (#636, #639).
- **Glow and blended parts in the World.** Colour tracks are not applied as
  tints or alpha, so a blended part can draw more opaque than in the client.
  Glows are drawn unlit, so glow batches not marked unlit draw brighter at
  night than in the client. Blended parts sort per part within a creature, so
  overlapping parts can sort wrongly from some angles. A creature shown in its
  rest pose draws its camera-facing glow and blended quads fixed. Fading
  weights are taken at time 0 of Stand, so a glow that pulses or fades during
  Stand stays at that value. On models whose Stand plays from an external
  skeleton, a keyed weight is decided only when every keyed slice starts full,
  the model has no sequences or global sequences of its own, and the track
  uses step or linear keys on sequence time (no global clock); the others are
  still left out, as are
  fading weights on creatures in the rest pose. Weight tracks are not read from
  `.anim` files or a parent skeleton (#361, #623, #625, #628, #636).
- **Bone files and parent skeletons.** Race and character models play their
  Stand on the base rig: the face bone files a Face choice selects are not
  applied, so a face can differ slightly from the client's, and the page says
  which one is left out. A child skeleton's parent is not read, and the page
  says so. `.anim` files are not read (#384, #680, #681, #684).
- **Sun direction.** The light's direction is still fixed. The light tables
  record colours, not a direction, and the client's sun path is not pinned yet
  (#177).
- **Water depth.** The shallow-to-deep mix reads the depth byte linearly; the
  client's exact curve and water textures are not used yet (#594).
- **View distance.** Near stays the default; whether Far becomes the default is
  not decided (#173).

  nearest tiles get NPCs, because creatures share 40 MiB of the objects
  budget (#726, #734, #735, #742, #755). The spawn marker's hover text still
  describes the client record's display only, so it can differ from the NPC
  drawn (#713).
- **Walk and fly.** Slope and step-up limits are estimates, not yet measured
  in the game, and so are the depth at which swimming starts and the margin
  for walking out (`SWIM_DEPTH_FRACTION`, `SWIM_EXIT_MARGIN`). Swimming has
  no buoyancy and no jump out of the water, so against a vertical quay wall
  a swimmer can only leave at steps, a ramp or a bank. The old MCLQ chunk
  liquid is not read, and building water under a tilted building is left
  out. Building water is always drawn with the deep water colour, because
  its data records no depth. Classic Era has no player flying, so flight
  controls are an approximation. The gryphon stores no hover or idle-in-air
  sequence, so it plays Fly even when still, and its capsule (2.3 yd radius,
  5.35 yd tall) does not fit doorways or most interiors. Building water that
  two tiles both list is stored and counted against the objects budget by
  each, though drawn once (#739). Jumps keep their
  take-off speed with no air steering, and neither body stores jump or fall
  sequences, so none are played. Collision
  covers the 3x3 tiles around the body and is read from every placement, so
  it does not depend on what is drawn; the collision cache is capped at
  96 MiB (Stormwind measured about 30 MiB). Right after arriving, buildings
  and doodads that have not loaded yet do not block; the status line says so
  (#704, #706, #711).
  With native presentation on Windows (`-Dvimi.world.nativePresent=true`),
  the standing murloc's idle may not refresh while the camera is still; this
  was not checked on Windows (#711).
- **NPC races without starting clothes.** Races without a playable bit have no
  new-character loadout, so they are shown without starting clothes; the page
  says so (#688).
- **Races without textures.** 15 of the 116 race variants are shown
  untextured, each with its reason on the page: 9 female NPC-race models
  that record no skin option (their male counterpart's skin is not borrowed,
  because the client's choice is not known), and the Grotto Netherwing
  Drake, Algarian Stormrider and Flourishing Whimsydrake (two variants each),
  whose surfaces use material setups that are not reviewed. Counts assume the
  needed texture files are stored; a race whose base skin file is missing
  says so instead of drawing a black body (#725).
- **Assumptions in the Races preview.** A texture weight stored outside the
  model is assumed fully opaque in the preview only, and environment
  reflections on two-texture surfaces are not drawn; the page states both.
  On 1.60.1.70862 every texture weight on a textured race is proven from keys
  stored in the model (22 of 55 tracks located through the skeleton's
  sequences), so the first assumption is not used (#685, #690).
- **Encrypted rows.** Humanoid NPC appearances need the community TACT keys:
  with the TACTKeys source turned off, a few encrypted rows in the NPC
  appearance tables stay hidden and those NPCs are shown untextured (#693).
- **macOS:** packages are unsigned and not notarized, and native presentation is
  not available. The signature check on `--image-only` builds was tested with
  `codesign` stubbed and has not been run on a Mac yet (#568).
- **Linux:** packages need the standard GTK and X11 desktop libraries, and native
  presentation is not available. The deb dependencies added in alpha.29 were
  not tested with a clean install.
