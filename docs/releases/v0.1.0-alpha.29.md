# VibeMiner v0.1.0-alpha.29

This update makes the World Explorer's light, water and terrain closer to the
game's. With the recorded sky on, terrain, buildings, doodads and the distant
horizon now share the zone's recorded light, and water takes its recorded ocean
and river colours, shallow to deep. Lava, fel and tar are drawn, in their own
colours and opaque. On Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor in build 1.60.1.70862,
every terrain chunk that declares texture layers is now textured. Glass and other see-through surfaces within one
object are drawn back to front, more creatures resolve their body parts, and a
streaming race that could end a World session is fixed. Library storage did not
change: no schema version or table changed since alpha.28, so an alpha.28
library opens as it is, without migration.

The shader changes for light, water and splat, and the see-through ordering,
passed the GPU proofs on Vulkan on Linux. The texture and creature changes were
measured on real data with the measurement probe and covered by unit tests,
without a new GPU proof. The Direct3D 11 shaders were built and verified offline
for every shader change, but the owner's on-screen checks on Windows are still
pending, the native window path can only run on Windows and has not been run
yet, and nothing new was run on Metal; see Known limits at the end.

## World Explorer: terrain textures

On Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor (maps 0 and 1) in build 1.60.1.70862, every
terrain chunk that declares texture layers is now drawn textured; other maps
were not counted. On Eastern Kingdoms (map 0, all 736 tiles) the last 33
untextured chunks, in six partly textured tiles, are gone; Kalimdor was already
fully textured.

- Uncompressed (BGRA) BLP textures decode with any defined alpha depth and any
  alpha encoding byte. Depth 0 decodes as opaque (#569).
- Non-square terrain diffuses, such as the two 768 × 128 files on map 0, are
  drawn stretched across the chunk, as the client draws them, instead of being
  refused (#569, #576). The resampling is an exact box filter, and the splat path
  follows the same shape rule as the baked atlas (#589).
- Terrain texture files with an empty texture list are read as chunks with no
  layers. The 24 Eastern Kingdoms tiles that were refused as a texture-file error
  are now reported as having nothing to draw; they look the same (#572).
- A chunk with no layers keeps its shadow bits (#577). Follow-up tests cover
  geometry reuse and flat-ground normals on lit terrain (#584).

Splat blending, which stays opt-in, is closer to being turned on by default:

- The terrain atlas pool is bounded by retained bytes, about 110 MiB, as well as
  by entry count. With splatting on and high masks it could hold about 171 MiB
  before (#583).
- The splat shader samples only in-range layers, its fade into the atlas no
  longer moves while you turn, and at coarser mask levels it no longer bleeds a
  neighbouring chunk's mask into the fade band (#581).

## World Explorer: water and liquids

Magma, fel and tar layers are drawn, in their LiquidType colour (orange, green
and near-black) (#586). Before, they were left out. On a sample of every fourth
tile of Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, all declared liquid layers are now drawn,
against 99.9% before. A layer in one of these formats is now drawn by its own
LiquidType row, so the same layer draws the same way in every tile (#588).
Magma, fel and tar are drawn opaque instead of see-through (#594).

Water can be shaded from the recorded water light (#587, #594). The client's
ocean and river colours, near and far, and their shallow and deep opacities are
read from the light tables. With the recorded sky on, the sea takes the zone's
ocean colours and lakes and rivers take the river colours, mixed by depth.
Without it, water draws exactly as before. On 1.60.1.70862, 1,890 of the 6,008
light records with readable water fields carry water colours; elsewhere water stays the default
teal. Tests cover blending, an unreadable colour and a missing opacity (#590),
and the depth-aware water merge (#603).

## World Explorer: light and sky

With the recorded sky on, the light recorded for the camera's place now reaches
the whole scene. Without it, the scene keeps the fixed default light:

- Terrain is lit with the recorded direct and ambient light for the camera's
  place and time of day (#591).
- Buildings, trees and props take the same World light as the terrain (#592).
  With the fixed default light they look exactly as before.
- The distant WDL horizon is lit by the same light as near terrain instead of
  its own baked light, so it follows the recorded light and darkens with the
  terrain at night (#597). With the default light, flat horizon ground is a
  little darker, in step with near terrain. A test pins which way its normals
  lean (#600). Turning terrain lighting off also leaves the horizon unlit.
- With native presentation into a child window on Windows, the recorded sky
  colours and fog are now drawn, as they are with the default presentation
  (#567).

## World Explorer: buildings, doodads and rendering

- Within one object, see-through ranges such as several glass or water panes
  are drawn back to front from the camera, so a near pane is no longer covered
  by one behind it (#574). The sort of the frame's see-through objects no longer
  allocates (#595).
- The World stream renderer checks its index buffers and the horizon's buffers
  for leaks, plans fragmented terrain meshes in fixed memory, and decides once
  per frame whether the horizon is drawn, so fog and horizon can no longer
  disagree for a frame (#571).

## World Explorer: creatures

Creatures stay opt-in. When a creature model records no geoset data, each geoset
group above 0 now draws its x01 section by default instead of x00, while group
0 still draws section 0 (#599). On
1.60.1.70862 the old rule refused 190 of 1,047 such models, because almost no
group has an x00 section; now 2 are refused. This default comes from the data
and matches the character path; it is not yet confirmed against the client.

The measurement probe gained a creature display census of Stand poses and
geoset outcomes over the whole build (#598, #602). Display models now have a
test for the unreviewed table shape refusal (#582).

## World Explorer: streaming, HUD and audio

- A tile whose preparation was refused, for example an unreadable or missing
  tile, could sometimes end the World session as "World streaming failed" when
  the helper's cancel reply arrived first. The refusal is now kept, and the tile
  shows as unavailable or is retried (#601).
- When the camera comes to rest, tiles load nearest first around it, instead of
  ahead along the direction it was moving (#575).
- An Error while loading minimap artwork now fails the request and is logged,
  instead of being shown silently as unavailable artwork (#565).
- F12 can no longer get stuck on "Screenshot still saving" if the writer thread
  cannot start, and a failed Open folder on the screenshot toast now says "Could
  not open the folder" (#579, #596).
- On a track change, the next World music or ambience track starts at once,
  instead of waiting up to 2 seconds for a look-ahead that ignores cancellation
  (#578).
- Test follow-ups for the stream controller and transport (#573, #604).

## Storage and performance

Library storage is unchanged: no schema, user version or table changed since
alpha.28. BLTE reads reuse a bounded pool of zlib inflaters instead of creating
one per chunk (#570, #593).

## Packaging and build

- Linux: the deb package's dependencies are mapped from the libraries the
  bundled natives actually need, and packaging fails on one that no declared
  package provides. This adds `libharfbuzz0b`, `libstdc++6` and `libgcc-s1`, and
  recommends `libvulkan1` (#563).
- macOS: bundle and native signatures are verified on `--image-only` builds too
  (#568, #593).
- The `verifyGlobalCandidateShaders` task is listed with the other verification
  tasks (#564).

## Measurement and tests

The read-only measurement probe, which runs only on a marked copy of a library,
gained a water mode (`-Pprobe.mode=water`) (#585) and a whole-build creature
display mode (`-Pprobe.mode=displays`) (#598, #602). The measurement baseline
now says to compare its 1.60.1.70862 Linux numbers by count, not time, against
the older Windows baseline (#580). The blob recovery test uses the shared
kill-point helper (#566).

## Known limits

- **Owner checks on Windows.** The shader changes (#581, #592, #594, #597) and
  the see-through ordering (#574) passed the GPU proofs on Vulkan on Linux. The
  texture and creature changes (#572, #576, #599) were measured on real data
  with the measurement probe and covered by unit tests, without a new GPU proof.
  The native window path (#567) can only run on Windows, and its proof has not
  been run there yet. The Direct3D 11 shaders were built in CI and verified
  offline, but these on-screen checks on Windows with Direct3D 11 are still
  pending:
  - the stretched non-square textures and the formerly untextured tiles (#576,
    #572);
  - water and lava: recorded ocean and river colours, coasts, and opaque lava,
    fel and tar (#586, #594);
  - the recorded light on terrain, objects and the horizon, by day and at night
    (#591, #592, #597);
  - glass and water panes in one object drawing in the right order (#574);
  - creatures' body parts under the new geoset default (#599);
  - the recorded sky in native window presentation (#567);
  - splat blending with `-Dvimi.world.splat=true`: the fade band staying put
    while turning, no seam from a neighbouring chunk, and no tiles stuck
    untextured on the high tier at Near (#581, #583);
  - ambience starting without an extra pause when flying between zones (#578).

  The Metal shaders are built, and nothing new was run on macOS hardware.
- **Sun direction.** The light's direction is still fixed. The light tables
  record colours, not a direction, and the client's sun path is not pinned yet
  (#177).
- **Water depth.** The shallow-to-deep mix reads the depth byte linearly; the
  client's exact curve and water textures are not used yet (#594).
- **Opt-in features.** These are Java system properties for testing, not
  settings in the app yet:
  - splat blending: `-Dvimi.world.splat=true`;
  - the recorded sky, fog, light and water light: `-Dvimi.world.sky=recorded`,
    with `-Dvimi.world.sky.time=HH:MM` to pin a time;
  - the Far view distance: `-Dvimi.world.viewDistance=far`;
  - creatures: `-Dvimi.world.creatures=true`;
  - WMO vertex colours: `-Dvimi.world.wmoVertexColours=true`;
  - high object texture detail: `-Dvimi.world.objectTextures=high`;
  - native presentation into a child window, on Windows only:
    `-Dvimi.world.nativePresent=true`.

  Terrain lighting is on and can be turned off with
  `-Dvimi.world.terrainLighting=false`.
- **Outland and Northrend** (maps 530 and 571) are not in build 1.60.1.70862,
  so the texture fixes could not be measured there.
- **NPCs.** On 1.60.1.70862, few spawned NPCs have a Creature record, so few
  reach a display, as in alpha.28.
- **macOS:** packages are unsigned and not notarized, and native presentation is
  not available. The signature check on `--image-only` builds was tested with
  `codesign` stubbed and has not been run on a Mac yet (#568).
- **Linux:** packages need the standard GTK and X11 desktop libraries, and native
  presentation is not available. The new deb dependencies were not tested with
  a clean install.
