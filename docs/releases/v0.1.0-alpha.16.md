# VibeMiner v0.1.0-alpha.16

World Explorer gains a north-up minimap in the top-right corner. Recorded artwork
scrolls beneath a centered player arrow that tracks facing. Artwork loads in the
background with its own activity indicator; missing regions show an honest
unavailable/partial state.

Flying now follows the correct terrain neighborhood. The previous camera lookup
swapped filename axes, which could unload approached terrain, show persistent blue
sky after loading, and restore it only when moving back. The continent grid keeps
its existing orientation. Space or E raises the camera; Q lowers it. Space and E
together keep the same ascent speed.

Maps without recorded terrain or with unavailable inventories explain the outcome
directly in the map panel. Positively recorded global-world-model maps explain
that model previews are not available yet; terrain-dependent actions are disabled.

Swift Timber Wolf (Mount105) and Swift Brown Ram (Mount94) regain their missing
reflective neck/armor sections in the normal preview. Their narrowly reviewed
legacy material profiles now bind the recorded textures. Appearance caches refresh
automatically; the diagnostic all-surfaces toggle has a clearer tooltip. Separate
unsupported additive sections remain explicitly partial.

Reviewed Dwarf male/female body and hair profiles now include textured hair and
the male beard cutout. Unreviewed auxiliary sections remain explicit partial
results. Existing Human, Orc and Tauren profiles retain their behavior.

Build Comparison adds **Item effects**, using prepared local data to compare
observed item links and qualified numeric inputs. Missing targets remain unknown;
observed-only records do not establish added/removed effects. Spell descriptions
and gameplay calculations remain unassessed. Results are session-only.

Terrain remains height-shaded with bounded nearby coverage. Greater view distance,
terrain textures, doodads, buildings and water are prioritized follow-up work.
Actual GPU rendering evidence covers Linux Vulkan; Metal/D3D hardware qualification
remains outstanding. This is an explicit prerelease after packaged checks pass.
